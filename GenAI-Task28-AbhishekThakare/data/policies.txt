Personal Knowledge Assistant - Expanded Policy Reference

This file goes into more detail on the same HR and IT policies covered in
notes.txt, mainly so there's enough text across the two files to actually
need chunking instead of fitting in one small block.

Leave Policy - Full Details
Full-time employees accrue 18 paid leaves per calendar year, credited at the
start of the year rather than month by month. Unused leaves up to a maximum
of 8 days can be carried forward into the next calendar year; anything above
that is forfeited. Leave requests go through the HR portal and need at least
2 working days' notice for planned leave. Sick leave taken without notice is
allowed but should be regularized on the portal within 3 working days of
returning, along with a doctor's note if the absence was longer than 2 days.
Maternity and paternity leave follow separate statutory policies and are not
deducted from the standard 18-day pool.

Reimbursement Policy - Full Details
Eligible expense categories are: local travel for work purposes, internet
bills for employees on a remote or hybrid arrangement, and one-time
work-from-home equipment purchases (chair, monitor, keyboard) up to a fixed
annual cap set by HR. Bills must be submitted through the HR portal within 30
days of the expense date; anything submitted later needs manager approval to
be considered. Once approved, reimbursement is paid out in the next payroll
cycle, not immediately. International travel expenses follow a separate
travel policy and are not covered by this general reimbursement process.

IT Support - Full Details
Standard laptop, monitor, and peripheral issues go through the internal IT
helpdesk ticketing system, with a target first response time of 4 working
hours. VPN access issues are also handled through the same ticketing system.
Password resets are entirely self-serve through the SSO portal and do not
require a ticket at all. For anything classified as critical - complete
system downtime, inability to log in to any company system, or a suspected
security incident - employees should call the IT helpline directly instead of
waiting in the regular ticket queue, since critical issues are handled
separately from the standard queue.

Onboarding Process - Full Details
The onboarding process spans the first 3 working days for every new hire,
regardless of department. Day 1 is handled entirely by IT: laptop handover,
VPN configuration, email and SSO account creation. Day 2 is handled by HR:
a walkthrough of the leave policy, reimbursement process, and the code of
conduct training requirement. Day 3 is handled by the new hire's reporting
manager and is specific to their project or team - this is the only day of
onboarding that isn't standardized across the company.

Code of Conduct Training - Full Details
Every employee must complete the annual code of conduct training within 30
days of their joining date, tracked automatically by HR through the training
portal. The training covers expected workplace behavior, data confidentiality
obligations, and how to report a conflict of interest. Managers receive an
automatic reminder if a direct report hasn't completed the training within 20
days of joining, so it rarely goes unnoticed, but it remains the employee's
own responsibility to complete it on time.
