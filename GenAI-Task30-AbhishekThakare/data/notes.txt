Personal Knowledge Assistant - Internal Notes

What is a Personal Knowledge Assistant?
A Personal Knowledge Assistant is the small project I've been building across these
GenAI assignments. The idea is simple: instead of a new employee searching through five
different HR and IT documents to find one answer, they just ask the assistant a question
in plain English and it pulls the answer from the company's internal documents.

Employee Onboarding Process
New employees at the company go through a 3-day onboarding process. Day 1 covers laptop
setup, VPN access, and email account creation, handled by the IT team. Day 2 covers HR
policies - leave policy, reimbursement process, and code of conduct. Day 3 is project-specific
training handled by the employee's reporting manager.

Technologies used for employee onboarding
The onboarding portal is built using React on the frontend and a Python FastAPI backend.
Employee data is stored in SQL Server. Login is handled through company SSO. The Personal
Knowledge Assistant chatbot itself is being prototyped using LangChain, with documents stored
in a FAISS vector store so the assistant can answer onboarding-related questions instantly.

Leave Policy
Employees get 18 paid leaves per calendar year, plus public holidays as per the state
calendar. Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 days in advance through the HR portal,
except in case of a medical emergency, where it can be informed after the fact along with
a doctor's note.

Reimbursement Process
Employees can claim reimbursement for approved work-related expenses (travel, internet bill
for remote work, and work-from-home equipment) by submitting bills on the HR portal within
30 days of the expense. Reimbursements are processed within one payroll cycle after approval.

IT Support
For laptop or VPN issues, employees should raise a ticket on the internal IT helpdesk portal.
Password reset requests are self-serve through the SSO portal and do not need a ticket.
Critical issues (system down, cannot login at all) can be escalated by calling the IT helpline
directly instead of waiting for a ticket to be picked up.

Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to complete the annual code of conduct training within 30 days of
joining. This covers workplace behavior, data confidentiality, and conflict of interest
guidelines. The training is mandatory and tracked by HR.
